STRATEGY COMPARISON (observed churn among members each strategy would contact)
------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Blanket (everyone): contacts=485 (100%), churners=55, churn rate=11.3% (95% CI 8.7%-14.2%), lift=1.0x, reaches 100% of all churners
2. Model top 20%, any member (incl. already lapsed): contacts=97 (20%), churners=38, churn rate=39.2% (95% CI 29.9%-48.5%), lift=3.5x, reaches 69% of all churners
3. Model top 20%, reachable members only: contacts=97 (20%), churners=22, churn rate=22.7% (95% CI 15.5%-30.9%), lift=2.0x, reaches 40% of all churners
4. Support outreach (reachable, >=1 late-window ticket): contacts=168 (35%), churners=30, churn rate=17.9% (95% CI 12.5%-23.8%), lift=1.6x, reaches 55% of all churners
5. Targeted coupon (reachable, model-flagged): contacts=78 (16%), churners=22, churn rate=28.2% (95% CI 17.9%-38.5%), lift=2.5x, reaches 40% of all churners
6. Support outreach + model-flagged (reachable): contacts=49 (10%), churners=18, churn rate=36.7% (95% CI 24.5%-51.0%), lift=3.2x, reaches 33% of all churners

LIMITATIONS
- Effectiveness values are assumptions; no campaign has been run. A randomised pilot is needed.
- Groups are small (see confidence intervals); results are indicative.
- Churners in the group are counted from known outcomes, so this is a retrospective simulation.
- Already-lapsed members (no purchase for 2+ months) churn almost certainly; they need a win-back offer, not prevention.
